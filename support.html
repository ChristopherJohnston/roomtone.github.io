Support

Email support@roomtone.cloud, and say which device you're using and what happened.

Where does my library live?

In folders on your Mac, which you add to Room Tone. Each song is a folder of its own: its voice memos, its DAW project, and its mixes and stem exports. Room Tone reads them where they are and doesn't move them. Notes, lyrics, ratings and comments are kept in a song.json file in each song's folder.

How do my songs get to my iPhone?

On the Mac, turn on Companion Sync at the bottom of the sidebar and choose iCloud Drive. It copies a preview of each version, and each song's details, to iCloud Drive/Room Tone. On the iPhone or iPad, choose that folder when Room Tone asks. After that, the Mac keeps it up to date, and what you change on the phone, such as tags, notes, lyrics and comments, goes back to the Mac.

How do I share a library with my band?

Each library has its own folder in iCloud Drive/Room Tone. Share that folder with your band from Finder or the Files app, and they choose it in Room Tone on their phones. They see that library's songs and nothing else of yours.

What does a share link do?

It opens a song in Room Tone, at the version and the moment it was sent from. It only works for someone who has the library it's in: if a bandmate doesn't, the link says which library it's from, so they can ask you to share its folder.

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 13 or later for your libraries, and an iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later. Apple Watch records ideas for the iPhone.
